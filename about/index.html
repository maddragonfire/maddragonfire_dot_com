About the craft

Arc, abrasive, judgment.

Mad Dragon Fire LLC is a Seattle metalworking shop focused on useful objects with visible evidence of the hands and heat that made them.

One at a time.

Each piece is fabricated individually. Dimensions can repeat, but the welds, grind marks, heat color, and small decisions never land in exactly the same place twice.

Material stays legible.

Steel should look like steel. Aluminum should look like aluminum. Finishing is used to clarify an object, not erase how it came together.

Useful before precious.

These are objects meant to hold phones, remotes, tools, notes, or whatever ends up on the workbench. Their weight and geometry are part of the experience.

Made in Seattle.

Every weld, grind, and finish is completed by hand by Mad Dragon Fire LLC. No two pieces are exactly alike.
