About the craft

Marine Veteran. Software Engineer. Artist. Welder.

Veteran ownership

Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business.

Mad Dragon Fire LLC is owned and operated by Skip Lester, a U.S. Marine Corps veteran. Our service-disabled veteran-owned small business (SDVOSB) status is reported in SAM.gov.

Every purchase and commission supports an independent, veteran-owned metalworking business in Seattle.
