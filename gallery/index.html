Shop fabrication · Steel

Custom Steel Welding Cart

A hand-fabricated workshop cart built from 1-inch steel angle with a 1/8-inch thickness and expanded-metal shelving, on a 1-foot by 2-foot footprint. The open frame provides an elevated work surface and a lower shelf, with 3 feet of space between them for equipment and supplies.

The 6-inch legs have casters and adjustable bolt feet for positioning and leveling. Made in the Mad Dragon Fire Seattle shop, fitted to the needs of a working workshop.
