Custom commissions

Bring the useful thing you cannot find.

A commission can begin with a dimension, a material, a broken part, or a rough sketch. The goal is a singular object that earns its place.

Start by email

01 · Idea

Use comes first.

Share what the object needs to hold, solve, fit, or survive. A photo or rough measurement is enough to begin.

02 · Scope

Material, finish, price.

We settle the dimensions, material, visual direction, schedule, and shipping before fabrication starts.

03 · Make

One object at a time.

The piece is cut, welded, ground, and finished by hand. Progress photos can be included when useful.

Good starting point

Say what you know.

Send the intended use, rough size, preferred metal, deadline, and any reference photos. Unknowns can be worked through together.
